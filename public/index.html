Love Community Garden is a garden in Nelson neighborhood in Muskegon, MI that bring neighbors together to grow food, build skills, and share produce. We run programs for seniors and youth, host a giving table for neighbors to share resources, and create a welcoming space for connection and growth. A social experiment in community care, healthy food access, and joyful gardening.

SPoP

Senior Power of Produce

Since 2016, the Senior Power of Produce (SPoP) program has welcomed nearly 2,000 seniors (age 60+) each week to the Muskegon Farmers Market from mid-July through August. Participants receive weekly vouchers—$6 in 2023—to purchase fresh, healthy produce, helping promote better nutrition and overall wellness.

While the program is not income-based, it plays an important role in addressing food insecurity and malnutrition among older adults. In addition to fresh produce, seniors can connect with local organizations offering valuable resources and support services at the market.

SPoP is made possible through the leadership of the Fruitport/Shoreline Lions Club , dedicated volunteers, and strong partnerships with the City of Muskegon , Senior Resources (Senior Millage) , and surrounding communities. Beyond nutrition, the program has become a meaningful social experience that many seniors look forward to each week.

Love Youth

Youth Learning, Fun & Growth

Love Community Garden offers a variety of youth programs designed to engage children and teens in health, growing food, building community connections, and having fun. Through hands-on activities, family get togethers, back to school supplies, and more, we aim to inspire a love for gardening, healthy living, and community care among our youth. Our programs are open to all local youth and are offered free of charge thanks to the support of our donors and volunteers.
