Growing people.
Growing community.

Love Community Garden was founded by 4 passionate individuals who believed in the power of growing food and producing could be used to bring people together and create positive change. Morning, a local farmer, had a vision of a community garden that would not only provide fresh produce but also serve as a hub for education and connection. Jody, a mail carrier, saw the potential for the garden to be a place where people could come together and build relationships. Bob, a retired gentleman, who just wanted to disrupt the status quo and create something meaningful in his community. And Art a retired architect turned artist, who saw a chance to design a real space for creativity and expression.

Our Mission

Our mission is to grow food, people, and connections in the Nelson Neighborhood of Muskegon, MI. We believe that by providing access to fresh produce and creating a space for education and connection, we can empower individuals and strengthen our community. Through our programs like SPoP, Love Youth, and the Giving Table, we aim to foster a sense of belonging and promote healthy living for all members of our community.
